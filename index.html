HomeCircle for iPhone and Android

HomeCircle is a recovery companion app for people in AA and NA. Find nearby meetings, connect with a supportive community, keep a private journal on your phone, and get gentle encouragement from the Recovery Coach whenever you need it.

  • Meetings near you – AA and NA meetings from public listings, shown by distance in km.
  • Your community – share posts, follow others and send support signals, only when you choose to.
  • Private journal – stays on your device and is never uploaded.
  • Recovery Coach – an AI helper for reflection and encouragement.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling your data.

Support Privacy Policy

Need help right now? HomeCircle is not an emergency service. In an emergency call 000. For crisis support call Lifeline on 13 11 14.
