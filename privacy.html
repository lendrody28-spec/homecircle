HomeCircle Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

This policy explains what information the HomeCircle app collects, how it is used and the choices you have. It applies to HomeCircle on iOS (bundle ID au.endrody.homecircle) and Android.

HomeCircle is operated by LJ Endrody, Endrody Collective, Australia ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email lulu.endrody@agentmail.to.

The short version

  • Your journal stays on your device. It is never uploaded.
  • Sensitive profile details (sober date, badges, faith, culture) are private or "Don't share" by default.
  • We use your approximate location on your device to show nearby meetings. We don't sell it.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps and websites, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time.

1. Information we collect

Account details

To create an account you sign up with your email address or phone number. We send you a one-time verification code to confirm it's you. We use these details to sign you in, keep your account secure and contact you about your account if needed.

Profile information you choose to add

  • Display name – the name other members see.
  • Recovery fellowship – for example AA or NA, so we can show relevant meetings and content.
  • Sober date and badges (optional) – private by default. They are only shown to others if you choose to share them.
  • Faith / beliefs and Culture (optional) – set to "Don't share" by default. You decide whether to fill these in and who can see them.

Some of this information, such as your recovery fellowship, sober date, faith and culture, may be sensitive information under Australian privacy law. We only collect it if you choose to provide it, and you can change or remove it in the app at any time.

Location

If you allow it, HomeCircle uses your device's approximate location to show meetings near you and their distance in kilometres. This is worked out on your device. You can still search for meetings without sharing your location, and you can turn location access off at any time in your device settings. We do not sell your location or use it for advertising.

Community activity

When you use the community feed, the posts you write, the people you follow and the support signals you send are stored so the community features work. This content is only shared to the extent you choose – please think about what you post, as other members can see shared posts.

Private journal

Your journal entries are stored on your device only. They are not uploaded to our servers and we cannot read them. If you delete the app or reset your device without a backup, your journal entries may be lost.

2. Recovery Coach (AI helper)

The Recovery Coach is an AI helper that offers reflection prompts and encouragement. It is not a counsellor, doctor or sponsor.

  • The Coach uses the profile preferences stored on your device (such as your fellowship) to tailor its replies.
  • It only uses a journal entry if you turn on "Share with Recovery Coach" for that specific entry. This setting is off by default for every entry.
  • When you send a message to the Coach, your message and any context you've chosen to share are sent to an AI service provider to generate a reply. These requests are processed only to produce the Coach's response and are not used for advertising.
  • The AI service provider is named in the app. [Provider details: TO BE UPDATED]

Please avoid sharing details in Coach chats that you would not want processed by the AI service.

3. Notifications

Notifications are off by default. If you turn them on, we use your device's push notification service to send reminders or community updates you've asked for. You can turn them off at any time in the app or your device settings.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information.

5. How we share information

We only share personal information:

  • with other HomeCircle members, when you choose to share it (for example a post or your display name);
  • with service providers who help us run the app (such as hosting, sign-in verification and the AI service for the Recovery Coach), only as needed to provide those services;
  • if required by law, or to protect the safety of a person.

Some service providers may store or process information outside Australia. Where this happens we take reasonable steps to make sure your information is handled in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

6. Meeting listings

Meeting information in HomeCircle comes from public sources, including BMLT (Basic Meeting List Toolbox), Meeting Guide, and public AA and NA meeting listings. Meeting times and locations can change, so please check with the group or local service body if you're unsure.

7. Keeping your information secure

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, including encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your device and sign-in details safe.

8. Your choices, access and deletion

  • You can view and update your profile in the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account and associated data in Settings within the app.
  • You can also ask us to delete your account and data, or to access or correct your information, by emailing lulu.endrody@agentmail.to.

When you delete your account, we delete your account and profile data and community content from our systems, except where we need to keep limited information to meet legal obligations. Journal entries live only on your device – delete the app to remove them.

9. Age requirement

HomeCircle is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Australian privacy law

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). If you have a privacy concern or complaint, please email us first and we will respond within a reasonable time (usually 30 days). If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make important changes, we'll let you know in the app or by updating the effective date on this page. Continuing to use HomeCircle after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Not affiliated with AA or NA

HomeCircle is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) or Narcotics Anonymous (NA).

Not a medical or emergency service

HomeCircle does not provide medical, psychological or emergency services. If you or someone else is in danger, call 000. For 24/7 crisis support, call Lifeline on 13 11 14.
