HomeCircle Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

This policy explains how the HomeCircle app handles your information and the choices you have. It applies to HomeCircle on iOS (bundle ID au.endrody.homecircle) and Android.

HomeCircle is operated by LJ Endrody, Endrody Collective, Australia ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email lulu.endrody@agentmail.to.

The short version

  • Everything you put into HomeCircle is stored on your phone. We don't collect it and we don't hold a copy.
  • Your journal stays on your device. It is never uploaded.
  • Sensitive profile details (sober date, badges, faith, culture) are private or "Don't share" by default.
  • Your approximate location is used on your phone to show nearby meetings. It is never sent to us.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps and websites, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and everything stored on your phone at any time.

1. Your information stays on your phone

HomeCircle is built so that what you enter stays on your phone. There's no HomeCircle account on our servers and we don't collect your data. The only time anything reaches us is if you choose to email us (see "Reporting and blocking" below).

Sign-up details

When you set up HomeCircle you can enter your email address or phone number, or skip this step. These details, and any verification code, stay on your phone. No SMS or email is sent, and no account is created on a server.

Profile information you choose to add

  • Display name – the name shown on your posts and profile.
  • Recovery fellowship – for example AA or NA, so the app can show relevant meetings and content.
  • Sober date and badges (optional) – private by default, and kept on your phone.
  • Faith / beliefs and Culture (optional) – set to "Don't share" by default. You decide whether to fill these in.

Some of this information, such as your recovery fellowship, sober date, faith and culture, may be sensitive information under Australian privacy law. It's entirely optional, it's stored on your phone only, and you can change or remove it in the app at any time.

Location

If you allow it, HomeCircle uses your device's approximate location to show meetings near you and their distance in kilometres. This is worked out on your phone and your location is never sent to us. You can still search for meetings without sharing your location, and you can turn location access off at any time in your device settings. We do not sell your location or use it for advertising.

Community activity

The posts you write, the people you follow, your messages and the support signals you send are stored on your phone. They aren't uploaded to our servers.

Reporting and blocking

To help keep HomeCircle a safe and supportive place, you can report posts, users and messages and block users right inside the app.

  • Reports are saved on your phone and you can send them to us by email at lulu.endrody@agentmail.to. From the Report sheet, your own mail app opens with the report filled in, and you choose whether to send it. We aim to review every report within 24 hours.
  • Blocking takes effect immediately on your phone.

A report you email us is the only time anything from HomeCircle reaches us, and it includes only what you choose to send. We use it only to look into the report and keep the community safe.

Private journal

Your journal entries are stored on your device only. They are never uploaded and we can't read them. If you delete the app or reset your device without a backup, your journal entries may be lost.

2. Recovery Coach

The Recovery Coach offers reflection prompts and a bit of encouragement along the way. It is not a counsellor, doctor or sponsor.

  • The Coach runs entirely on your phone. It uses simple built-in rules and templates to put together its replies – it is not an AI service.
  • The Coach makes no network calls and there is no third-party provider involved. Nothing you type to the Coach ever leaves your phone.
  • The Coach uses the profile preferences stored on your device (such as your fellowship) to tailor its replies.
  • It only uses a journal entry if you turn on "Share with Recovery Coach" for that specific entry. This setting is off by default for every entry, and even when it's on, that entry still stays on your device.

So you can chat with the Coach knowing your words stay with you, on your phone.

3. Notifications

Notifications are off by default. If you turn them on, HomeCircle schedules the reminders you've asked for on your phone. Nothing is sent from our servers. You can turn them off at any time in the app or your device settings.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information.

5. How we share information

Because your information stays on your phone, we don't have it to share. We don't use hosting, sign-in or other service providers that receive your data, and we never sell, rent or trade personal information.

If you email us a report or a question, we see only what you send. We use it to look into the report or reply to you, and we would only pass it on if required by law or to protect someone's safety. Emails to us are handled by our email provider, which may store them outside Australia; where this happens we take reasonable steps to make sure your information is handled in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

6. Meeting listings

Meeting information in HomeCircle comes from public sources, including BMLT (Basic Meeting List Toolbox), Meeting Guide, and public AA and NA meeting listings. Meeting times and locations can change, so please check with the group or local service body if you're unsure.

7. Keeping your information secure

Your information is stored on your phone and protected by your phone's own security, such as your passcode, Face ID and device encryption. Please keep your phone locked and up to date. We take reasonable steps to protect any emails you send us from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

8. Your choices, access and deletion

  • You can view and update your profile in the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app by going to Settings > Delete account. This removes everything HomeCircle has stored on your phone, including your profile, posts, messages and journal. We hold no copy, so there's nothing left on our side.
  • If you've emailed us a report or question and would like us to delete that email, or you have any other privacy question, email lulu.endrody@agentmail.to.

9. Age requirement

HomeCircle is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 is using HomeCircle, please let us know.

10. Australian privacy law

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). If you have a privacy concern or complaint, please email us first and we will respond within a reasonable time (usually 30 days). If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make important changes, we'll let you know in the app or by updating the effective date on this page. Continuing to use HomeCircle after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Not affiliated with AA or NA

HomeCircle is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) or Narcotics Anonymous (NA).

Not a medical or emergency service

HomeCircle does not provide medical, psychological or emergency services. If you or someone else is in danger, call 000. For 24/7 crisis support, call Lifeline on 13 11 14.
