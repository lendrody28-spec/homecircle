HomeCircle Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026 · Applies to HomeCircle 1.2.17

This policy explains how the HomeCircle app handles your information. It applies to HomeCircle on iOS (bundle ID au.endrody.homecircle) and Android.

HomeCircle is operated by LJ Endrody, Endrody Collective, Australia ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email lulu.endrody@agentmail.to.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. HomeCircle has no server that holds your data.
  • Your profile, journal, posts and messages are stored only on your phone. We don't collect them and we don't have a copy. In this version nothing is shared between phones.
  • Reports, blocks and mutes are saved on your phone only. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Kai, the Recovery Coach, uses built-in templates on your phone. It is not AI and makes no network calls.
  • The app downloads a public meeting list. That download sends no personal details.
  • Your location is used only when you tap Near me, and it stays on your phone.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps and websites, and we never sell your data.

1. Your information stays on your phone

HomeCircle is built so that what you enter stays on your phone. There's no sign-up, no email or phone number to give, no password and no verification code. Nothing you enter is sent to us.

Profile information you choose to add

  • Display name, shown on your posts and profile on your phone.
  • Recovery fellowship, for example AA or NA, so the app can show relevant meetings and content.
  • Sober date and badges (optional), hidden by default.
  • Faith / beliefs and Culture (optional), set to "Don't share" by default.

Some of this information may be sensitive information under Australian privacy law. It's optional, it's stored on your phone only, and you can change or remove it in the app at any time.

Journal, check-ins, posts and messages

Your journal, check-ins, posts, follows, messages and support signals are stored on your phone. They aren't uploaded to us and, in this version, they aren't sent to other people's phones.

Reporting, blocking and muting

You can report posts, people and messages, and block or mute people. These work on your phone to hide content. Reports are saved on your phone only. Nothing is sent to us and nobody at HomeCircle reviews reports. If someone may be in danger, call 000. Read our community rules in the app for more.

2. Kai, the Recovery Coach

Kai offers reflection prompts and encouragement. It is not a counsellor, doctor or sponsor.

  • Kai's replies are put together on your phone from built-in templates. It is not an AI service.
  • Kai makes no network calls and no third party is involved. Nothing you type to Kai leaves your phone.
  • Kai can use the preferences stored on your phone (such as your fellowship) to tailor its replies.

3. What the app downloads

Meeting list

To show meeting times, the app downloads a public list of AA and NA meetings from this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. Apart from map tiles on Android (below), this is the only thing the app downloads, and the request doesn't include any of your details. Like any website, the host (GitHub, in the United States) receives a standard web request, including your device's internet (IP) address. We don't receive or keep it.

Meeting information comes from public sources, including BMLT (Basic Meeting List Toolbox), Meeting Guide and public AA and NA meeting listings. Times and locations can change, so check with the group if you're unsure.

Map tiles (Android)

On Android, the meetings map may load map images ("tiles") from OpenStreetMap servers. Those servers see a standard web request, including your IP address and which area of the map is being shown. No personal details from HomeCircle are sent.

4. Location

HomeCircle uses your location only when you tap Near me, and only if you allow it. It's used on your phone to sort meetings by distance in kilometres and is never sent to us. You can search for meetings without sharing your location, and you can turn location access off at any time in your phone's settings.

If you tap Directions, the meeting's address opens in your maps app, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

5. Notifications

Notifications are off by default. If you turn them on, reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server.

6. Backups and crash reports

HomeCircle data may be included in your phone's normal backup (iCloud or your computer on iPhone, Google backup on Android) if you have backup turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under your account, and we can't access them.

If you've chosen to share diagnostics with Apple or Google in your phone's settings, they may share anonymous crash data with developers under their own policies.

7. HomeCircle Plus

Plus isn't on sale yet. If it launches, payment will be handled by Apple or Google and we'll never see your card details. We'll update this policy first.

8. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising services, and no AI service receives your data.
  • We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information.

9. Emails you send us

If you email us a question, we see only what you send and use it only to reply. We'd only pass it on if required by law or to protect someone's safety. Emails are handled by our email provider, which may store them outside Australia. You can ask us to delete your email at any time.

10. Keeping your information secure

Your information is protected by your phone's own security, such as your passcode, Face ID and device encryption. Please keep your phone locked and up to date.

11. Your choices: export, import and delete

Everything is on your phone, so you're in control. In the app, open Settings and go to Your data:

  • Export my data makes a file of your HomeCircle data on your phone. It stays on your phone until you choose to share or save it somewhere.
  • Import from file replaces the HomeCircle data on your phone with an exported file, after you confirm.
  • Delete account erases the HomeCircle data stored on your phone. There's no online account. This button only clears your phone, because we hold no copy.
  • Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone.

12. Age requirement

HomeCircle is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

13. Australian privacy law

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. If you have a privacy concern or complaint, please email us first and we'll respond within a reasonable time (usually 30 days). If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make important changes, we'll update the effective date on this page and let you know in the app.

15. Not affiliated with AA or NA

HomeCircle is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) or Narcotics Anonymous (NA).

Not a medical or emergency service

HomeCircle is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice. It doesn't provide medical, psychological or emergency services. If you or someone else is in danger, call 000. For 24/7 crisis support, call Lifeline on 13 11 14. For alcohol and drug advice, call the National Alcohol and Other Drug Hotline on 1800 250 015, or Adis in Queensland on 1800 177 833.
