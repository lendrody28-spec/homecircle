4. Code of conduct

HomeCircle helps you find meetings and look after your recovery. When you use the app, and at meetings you find through HomeCircle, please:

  • Be respectful. Treat others with kindness, even when you disagree. Everyone's recovery looks different.
  • No harassment. No bullying, threats, hate speech, discrimination, sexual harassment or unwanted contact.
  • No selling or supplying substances. Don't offer, sell, supply, swap or ask for alcohol, drugs, prescription medicines or anything else that could harm someone's recovery.
  • No scams or asking for money. Don't run scams, ask for money, loans, gifts or donations, or promote products or services.
  • No moving people to other apps to exploit them. Don't try to move people to other apps, sites or private channels to take advantage of, groom, pressure or scam them.
  • No sharing other people's details. Don't share anyone else's name, photo, contact details, location or story without their clear permission.
  • Respect meetings. At meetings you find through HomeCircle, please respect that group's own guidelines, and don't record or share what people say.
  • Nothing illegal or harmful. Don't use HomeCircle for anything unlawful, or to encourage self-harm or harm to others.
