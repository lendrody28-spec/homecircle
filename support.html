What is HomeCircle?

HomeCircle is a recovery companion app for people in AA and NA. It helps you find nearby meetings, connect with a supportive community, keep a private journal on your phone and get encouragement from the Recovery Coach, an AI helper. HomeCircle is independent and not affiliated with Alcoholics Anonymous or Narcotics Anonymous.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find meetings?

Open the Meetings tab. If you allow location access, HomeCircle shows AA and NA meetings near you sorted by distance in km. Listings come from public sources (BMLT, Meeting Guide and public AA/NA listings), so times can change – check with the group if you're unsure. Spotted a wrong listing? Email us and we'll look into it.

Is my journal private?

Yes. Your journal is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to our servers. The Recovery Coach can only see a journal entry if you turn on "Share with Recovery Coach" for that entry, and that's off by default.

Who can see my sober date, faith or culture?

No one, unless you choose to share. Sober date and badges are private by default, and Faith/beliefs and Culture are set to "Don't share" by default. You can change these any time in your profile.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account in Settings in the app. This deletes your account, profile and community content. You can also email lulu.endrody@agentmail.to from the email address (or with the phone number) linked to your account and ask us to delete it. Journal entries live on your device – delete the app to remove them.

How do I report a post or a user?

Use the Report option on the post or on the person's profile, or email lulu.endrody@agentmail.to with the person's display name, a description and a screenshot if you can. We review every report and remove content or accounts that break our community guidelines.

I didn't get my verification code

Check that your email or phone number is entered correctly, look in your spam or junk folder, and wait a minute before requesting a new code. If it still doesn't arrive, email us.

Is the Recovery Coach a real person?

No. The Recovery Coach is an AI helper for reflection and encouragement. It isn't a counsellor, doctor or sponsor and can make mistakes. If you're struggling, reach out to your sponsor, your doctor, or call Lifeline on 13 11 14.

Does HomeCircle show ads or sell my data?

HomeCircle has no ads, doesn't track you across other apps and never sells your data.

I need crisis help

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000. For 24/7 crisis support call Lifeline on 13 11 14, or Beyond Blue on 1300 22 4636.
